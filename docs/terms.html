Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-08-10

"The Unsolved Minute" is a personal content project that produces short-form video content (mystery and aviation history stories) for platforms including YouTube and TikTok. This app is used to automate script writing, footage sourcing, narration, and publishing of that content to the operator's own social media accounts.

Use of the app

This application connects to your own YouTube and/or TikTok account via each platform's official API to publish video content on your behalf. It is intended for personal use by the account owner only.

Content

Video content is produced using publicly available reference material and openly licensed imagery, with appropriate attribution included in video descriptions where required by the source license.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time as the project evolves.

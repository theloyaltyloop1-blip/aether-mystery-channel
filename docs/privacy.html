Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-10

"The Unsolved Minute" is a personal automation project. This policy explains what data the app handles when connected to your YouTube and/or TikTok account.

What data is accessed

The app uses OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos to, and read basic statistics from, the connected account. It does not access private messages, contacts, or any data unrelated to video publishing and video performance statistics.

What data is stored

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device to allow the app to publish videos without repeated logins. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Stored tokens can be revoked at any time via your YouTube/TikTok account's connected-apps settings, which immediately ends the app's access.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner via the associated GitHub repository.
